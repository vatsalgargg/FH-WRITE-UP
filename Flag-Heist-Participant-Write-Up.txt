FLAG HEIST - THE VEYRA BREACH
==============================

Veyra Cloud Solutions has been breached. Your job is to piece together
what happened by investigating 13 connected challenges. You will work
through websites, encrypted messages, public information, and digital
evidence. Each task has one flag in the format ROOT{...}.

GET STARTED

1. Import Flag-Heist-THM-cloud-ready.ova into Oracle VirtualBox.
2. Follow Flag-Heist-Local-VirtualBox-Steps.txt to set the VM to Host-only
   networking and find its IP address. This keeps the lab on your computer.
3. Start the VM and give the services about two minutes to come up.
4. On your computer, open http://VM_IP/ in your browser. Replace VM_IP
   with the IP of your local Flag Heist VM.
5. Sign in to the Mission Console using the operator account in the room
   instructions. Open Task 1 to start. Its direct URL is http://VM_IP:3101/.

The Mission Console lists the tasks, opens the challenge sites, and
provides the evidence files. Keep the Flag Heist VM running while you play.
Since this copy runs on your computer, you can skip the hosted AttackBox
and OpenVPN.

SUBMIT YOUR FLAGS

If your organiser is using the TryHackMe room for submissions, enter
each flag in the matching task there. Use the IP from your local VM for
the challenge sites. Use the room page to submit your answers.
